Method
Mix, ferment, refrigerate or freeze
  1. Mix everything until no dry flour remains. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Knead with the KitchenAid dough hook on speed 2 for about 5–7 minutes.
  3. Leave at room temperature for about 45–60 minutes.
  4. Divide into the number of balls shown above and round each tightly.
  5. Refrigerate balls you plan to use within about 1–3 days.
  6. Freeze the rest promptly in lightly oiled individual containers or freezer bags.
  7. For frozen dough, thaw in the refrigerator the day before and bring to room temperature 2–3 hours before baking.
